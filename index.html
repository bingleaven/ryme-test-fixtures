Lumen Grid - Seed Pitch Deck [SYNTHETIC TEST MATERIAL]

Lumen Grid builds software that helps small commercial buildings cut electricity bills by scheduling HVAC and lighting around real occupancy.

Fictional company created only to test a report pipeline.

Stage: seed. Team: 4 people. Based in a fictional city.

Slide 2: Problem

Small commercial buildings (10k to 50k sq ft) waste about 25 percent of their energy because equipment runs on fixed schedules.

Facility managers lack time and tools to tune schedules each season.

Existing building-automation systems cost 40k dollars or more to install.

Slide 3: Solution and Product

A 300 dollar sensor kit plus a cloud service learns occupancy patterns and adjusts HVAC and lighting schedules automatically each week.

Install takes under two hours with no rewiring of the main panel.

Pilot results: 18 percent average energy reduction across 12 buildings.

Slide 4: Market and Traction

Target: 5.6 million US commercial buildings under 50k sq ft.

Initial wedge: property managers with 10 to 100 buildings.

Traction: 12 paid pilots, 38k dollars ARR, 3 expansion commitments.

Net revenue retention in pilots: 118 percent (small sample).

Slide 5: Business Model

Subscription of 6 dollars per sensor per month plus a 300 dollar kit.

Gross margin about 68 percent at current scale.

Customer acquisition through two channel partners (fictional).

Payback period on acquisition cost: about 9 months.

Slide 6: Ask and Use of Funds

Raising 2.5 million dollars seed round (fictional).

Use: 50 percent engineering, 30 percent sales, 20 percent operations.

Milestones in 18 months: 120 buildings, 400k dollars ARR.

Key risks: sensor supply, long property-manager sales cycles.
